# Athena Security

> Athena Security is an AI-powered entryway security company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Austin, Texas, with offices in the San Francisco Bay Area and an Experience Center in Jacksonville, Florida. The company serves healthcare systems, K-12 school districts, casinos, government facilities, and corporate campuses across the United States and internationally. Athena's platform integrates concealed weapons detection, AI-assisted X-ray scanning, visitor management, holographic telepresence officers, and AR alert glasses into a single connected system. The company is NDAA compliant, supports California AB 2975 hospital weapons-detection compliance, has deployed across clients including Duke Health (21 screening locations) and Lamar Consolidated ISD (24 systems), and received the Best Weapons Detection System Award at the 2022 ASTORS Homeland Security Awards.

## Solutions

- [Concealed Weapons Detection System](https://www.athena-security.com/concealed-weapons-detection-system/): AI-powered walkthrough screening platform compatible with Apollo 500, Apollo 650, CEIA Opengate, Garrett, and Metrasens Ultra hardware that detects concealed ferrous and non-ferrous threats while allowing everyday items to pass through.
- [Hospital Visitor Management System](https://www.athena-security.com/hospitals): iPad-based visitor check-in system purpose-built for healthcare with real-time background checks, watchlist screening, Epic EHR integration, badge printing, and always-on offline capability during internet outages.
- [Ambulance Bay Weapons Detection System](https://www.athena-security.com/concealed-weapons-detection-system/ambulance-bay-weapons-detection/): Purpose-built screening for stretcher patients arriving at hospital EMS entry points, using non-ionizing imaging radar to detect metallic and non-metallic threats while ignoring the stretcher itself, with single-operator iPad console and automatic chain-of-custody documentation.
- [AI Assisted Baggage X-Ray](https://www.athena-security.com/baggage-xray-scanner/): Computer vision X-ray solution identifying firearms, knives, explosives, and 3D-printed weapons in real time, with automatic belt-stop technology and a single-console Workforce Multiplier configuration.
- [Telepresence Security Officer](https://www.athena-security.com/telepresence-security-officer/): Holographic security officer system allowing one remote operator to cover up to four entry points with life-size interactive projection, multi-language support, and live operator mode.
- [AthenaVision AR Alert Glasses](https://www.athena-security.com/ar-alert-glasses/): Augmented reality glasses delivering hands-free, real-time weapons detection and X-ray alerts to security officers so no notification is missed during active screening.
- [Active Shooter Training](https://www.athena-security.com/active-shooter-training/): Organizational preparedness training programs designed to complement Athena's detection technology by preparing staff and security personnel for active shooter scenarios.
- [DHS Best Practices Integration](https://www.athena-security.com/concealed-weapons-detection-system/dhs-best-practices/): Platform-level enforcement and documentation of Department of Homeland Security screening guidelines including DHS 14.2.2 prohibited items logging, DHS 8.2 equipment testing, and DHS 8.5 secondary screening procedures.
- [Compliance and Reporting](https://www.athena-security.com/compliance/): Athena's compliance documentation and reporting capabilities covering SOC 2, HIPAA considerations, and regulatory alignment including California AB 2975 for hospital weapons detection mandates.
