Summary: To write a school safety grant application for a weapons detection system, you must first read the grant requirements carefully, conduct a safety assessment to collect evidence, and describe the WDS you want to implement. Then, draft a detailed implementation plan with stakeholder support, measurable outcomes, and sustainability. Finally, build and justify your budget.
